SHAPWICK BUNNY HOP 1.4.3 — HQ CONTROLLER'S GUIDE

SET UP
Extract the complete ZIP to a folder on the Windows PC. Open BunnyHop.exe.
This is a portable application: no Python, internet connection, administrator
installation or command window is needed. Keep race files in a writable folder.
Excel is used to edit entry sheets and view results. The processor itself does
not require Excel. Allow a few seconds for the application to open.

BEFORE THE RACE
1. Click Browse and choose the original FOS Participants CSV or numbered Excel
   Master (.xlsx). Do not rename a CSV to an Excel extension.
2. Check Event year and race date. The date defaults to Easter Monday.
3. Click 1. CHECK ENTRANTS (BEFORE RACE).
4. In the same folder as your input, open BunnyHop_YEAR_Entry_Check.xlsx.
   Check Issues to Review. The companion BunnyHop_YEAR_Results_Entry.xlsx is
   the worksheet for race numbers and times.
5. Allocate unique race numbers manually if they are blank. Numbers already
   allocated in a Master are preserved. FOS does not allocate the bibs.

An Excel Master can be a standalone sheet with RaceNumber, Firstname, Lastname,
Gender, AgeCategory and optional ClubName, or the existing FOS workbook with
a second sheet named Master list. Master categories are preserved; available
age/DOB information is used to flag discrepancies. Raw FOS categories are
derived from race-day age and sex. Check any discrepancies before race day.

AFTER THE RACE
1. Enter finish times in Results Entry. Use the established minutes.seconds
   convention: 45.05 = 45 minutes 5 seconds; 45.10 or 45.1 = 45 minutes
   10 seconds; 45.5 = 45 minutes 50 seconds. 65.03 = 65 minutes 3 seconds.
   Leave Time blank for non-finishers. Do not enter finishing positions.
2. Save and close the workbook in Excel.
3. Browse to the completed .xlsx and click 2. PROCESS RESULTS (AFTER RACE).
4. Open BunnyHop_YEAR_Classified_Results.xlsx beside the input.
   Review Start Here and Data Checks, then the awards and full results.
   HQ Controller prepares results; Race Director approves final awards.

The historical All runners results input tab is recognised even when the
FOS export is the first sheet. Otherwise use Results Entry or put the input
table on the first sheet. Do not feed a published summary back as results input.

RESULTS AND AWARDS
Times determine every finishing position. Equal times retain source row order.
The first three men and first three women get overall prizes. Age-group prizes
pass to the first three eligible runners excluding overall prize winners;
true age positions remain unchanged. Categories remain SM, SF, MV40/50/60/70/80
and FV40/50/60/70. The oldest listed category includes older runners.
Shapwick and Street Striders tabs highlight the first male and female in yellow;
these local awards are independent of the overall/age prize pass-down.
Club spelling suggestions are not silently merged. Blank club is acceptable.

IF SOMETHING NEEDS ATTENTION
ERROR means HOLD publication and resolve the listed source data. REVIEW means
check the item. Correct the appropriate input, save it, and run again.
The processor preserves inputs and creates _v2, _v3, etc. instead of replacing
earlier output. Use the newest output. Close Excel if it is locking a file.
No contact, medical or address fields are copied to the generated race sheets.

Release acceptance status and remaining testing limits are in AUDIT_AND_TESTS.md.
